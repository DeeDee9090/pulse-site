WEEKLY MILESTONES

Your week,
already written

PULSE pulls your live MSX milestones and the week's real activity — meetings, emails, follow-ups — and drafts the weekly progress narrative for each one. You review, tweak, and save. It files everything by fiscal week automatically, with a roll-up of territory value, ACR, HoK and blockers across every deal.

  • AI-drafted weekly progress from your actual M365 activity
  • Direct / Partner / Internal / Cross-Territory evidence classification
  • Safe Account holding when genuine customer work cannot yet be linked to pipeline
  • Guarded milestone comments and HVA tasks, including explicit task duration
EDIT & PUSH TO CRM

Review, edit,
push to CRM

Every card opens into a full editor. Refine the weekly progress, set the status, capture next steps and add tasks — then inspect the exact account, opportunity, milestone, comment and task operation in the guarded preview. Confirmed operations execute as the signed-in user. The generated text is a starting point; the final word is always yours.

  • Editable commentary — the CRM comment updates as you type
  • Set milestone status: On Track, At Risk, Blocked, Completed…
  • HVA tasks include an explicit 15–120 minute duration (30 minutes by default)
  • All classified evidence can enter the guarded preview; nothing writes until you explicitly confirm
ACCOUNT LIST

Your whole
territory, mapped

PULSE reads your deal-team assignments straight from Dataverse and lays out your full book of business — parent accounts, their child organisations, and the territory each rolls up to. Duplicate records are de-duplicated, and each account is tagged with the SE motions in play so you always know your patch.

  • Parent accounts and child organisations from your deal teams
  • Grouped by territory unit, with sub-org counts
  • Motion tags per account so coverage is obvious
  • The same source of truth the Tracker and Whitespace use
SE EXECUTION TRACKER

Every gap,
made actionable

Tracker uses one evidence-led Stage 2–3 population and turns execution gaps into operational views. Start with the prioritised This Week queue, use Opportunity Review for structured deal inspection, and use Opportunity Radar to discover or qualify work that is not yet represented correctly.

  • This Week ranks the highest-value open execution actions
  • Opportunity Review shows ownership, coverage, POC/Pilot, HVA and Technical Win state
  • Opportunity Radar separates confirmed signals from qualification needs
  • Aligned-territory and cross-territory deal-team lenses preserve the correct scope
SE MOTION SCORECARD

Execution measures,
scored with context

The FY27 scorecard separates milestone engagement, POC/Pilot ownership, HVA evidence, Technical Win coverage, milestone progression and the governed Database, Security and Factory attach measures. Each card states its population so unlike denominators are not mixed into one percentage.

  • Current-quarter, completed-quarter, year-to-date and weekly-progress views
  • Measure-specific denominators and explicit exception rules
  • Opportunity-level evidence behind each result
  • Click through to inspect the actions behind the number
MOTION DRILL-IN

Attach gaps,
deal by deal

Click any motion and PULSE opens the deals behind the score — exactly which opportunities have it attached and which don't, each named with its ACR and the gap ACR still on the table. It's the difference between "we should attach more" and a named list of deals to act on this week.

  • Coverage % and gap ACR for every motion
  • Attached vs gap opportunities, named with their ACR
  • "Why this matters" context for each motion
  • Discipline-aware: your primary motions vs adjacent / hand-off
TERRITORY WHITESPACE

Where to build
pipeline next

Whitespace turns your territory into an action queue. PULSE finds accounts with an active opportunity but no matching motion (Attach Gaps) and accounts with no active opportunity at all (Greenfield), then ranks them by uncovered pipeline and how soon the anchor deal closes — and offers an AI-suggested attach play for each.

  • Attach Gap vs Greenfield, grouped by territory
  • Prioritised by pipeline size and closing urgency
  • Names the exact deal(s) to attach a motion to
  • One-click "Suggest attach play" — an AI next step grounded in the live deal
CAF NOMINATIONS

Never miss a
Factory nomination

PULSE watches the Cloud Accelerate Factory data and surfaces the opportunities that are eligible but not nominated, stalled, or declined. When one qualifies, it assembles the full nomination packet — mapped to the Azure Offer Navigator form — for you to review and submit.

  • Eligible / stalled / declined nomination queue
  • Auto-resolved specialist and co-sell partner
  • Copy-ready packet aligned to Azure Offer Navigator sections
  • PULSE never submits for you — you stay in control
FY CONNECT

Your Connect,
evidenced all year

PULSE discovers the scheduled Connect conversation and builds the correct evidence window around it. Review retrieved contributions, attribution gaps, financial context, FY27 goal measures and workstreams before asking AI to draft the narrative.

  • Scheduled First or Next Connect window, with pre-read deadline
  • Financial summary and ACR-by-customer, deduped per opportunity
  • Needs-review, attribution-pending and verified-history queues
  • Five-item pagination plus goal, workstream and date filters
AI-WRITTEN CONNECT

Reflect & plan,
written for you

After review, PULSE drafts your Connect from verified evidence — results you delivered and how, setbacks and what you learned, goals for the period ahead, and the behaviours that will get you there. Every claim is grounded in a real logged activity, with character counts and copy buttons ready for the review form.

  • "Reflect on the past" and "Plan for the future" in your voice
  • Grounded only in evidence you've logged — no invention
  • Character counts tuned to the Connect form limits
  • Copy each answer, or the entire Connect, in one click
EVIDENCE BY GOAL

Mapped to your
goals 1–5

Under the prose, verified evidence is classified by FY27 goal and consolidated workstream. Contribution type, source lineage, customer context and attribution status remain visible so every material claim can be traced and corrected.

  • Evidence classified to the appropriate FY27 goal or supporting-only context
  • "What (results)" and "How (behaviours)" per goal
  • Mandatory compliance remains separate from role and technical learning
  • Saved Connects are private, versioned snapshots

WHERE YOUR DATA COMES FROM

Your data, never invented

PULSE retrieves grounded data from MSX and the signed-in user's Microsoft 365. It never invents customers, opportunities, milestones or numbers, and any external write requires an explicit guarded confirmation.

🤝

Milestones, opportunities & commentary

Come from MSX for every account and opportunity where you're on the deal team (Account or Opportunity Team). Not on a deal team? It won't appear. Added to one? It shows up. PULSE surfaces and structures what MSX already holds — the weekly narrative is a draft you approve.

🗂️

Your account list

Comes from your MSX Account Team — the same source your RAIN profile uses. PULSE mirrors it exactly and de-duplicates, so your patch matches what MSX has on record.

🔍

Evidence — mail, chat, meetings

Pulled from your own Microsoft 365 for the selected Weekly or Connect evidence window. PULSE prioritises active contribution — work you sent, presented or authored — rather than passive attendance or copied messages.

⚠️ At fiscal-year crossover (early July) MSX re-provisions territories and account teams. For a short spell your account list can look larger, smaller, or spread across regions outside your patch until assignments settle — this is MSX data catching up, not a PULSE error. PULSE always reflects whatever your Account Team says today; if it still looks wrong once the dust settles, it's an account-team provisioning fix to raise with your MSX / ops contact.
