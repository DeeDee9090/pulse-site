WEEKLY MILESTONES

Prove the work before writing the update.

Weekly does far more than draft prose. It retrieves activity for an exact period, distinguishes customer outcomes from context, links evidence to the correct milestone, builds an editable draft and then prepares a guarded batch of append-only MSX updates.

  • Retrieve: refresh the signed-in portfolio and collect evidence for the selected dates.
  • Decide: verify, exclude or route every substantive contribution to Weekly / MSX, Account holding or Connect.
  • Generate: create milestone progress, outcomes, blockers, next steps and CRM-ready commentary from approved evidence.
  • Push safely: review every destination, append-only comment, warning and task before explicitly confirming the MSX batch.

FY27 CONNECT

Prepare the conversation—not just the prose.

Connect turns the whole year’s verified delivery into a coherent, defensible conversation. It uses the authoritative guidance for the signed-in CAIP, AIBS or Security profile, calculates the correct evidence window and keeps source proof, goals, workstreams, customer value and attribution visible before any narrative is generated.

  • Prepare: confirm the conversation, deadline, discipline context and exactly three business bets.
  • Prove: review new contributions, attribution-pending customer work and verified evidence history.
  • Measure: connect evidence to all five FY27 goals, financial context, HVA, Technical Win, workstreams and learning.
  • Tell the story: generate, edit and save a private versioned narrative without rewriting history.

REPORTS & VERSION HISTORY

Keep the evidence and the decision that existed at the time.

PULSE does not silently regenerate history from today’s changed portfolio. Reports reopens the saved Weekly period or private Connect version with its originating evidence and snapshot context intact.

  • Saved Weekly drafts and reviewed milestone updates
  • Private versioned Connect narratives
  • Historical portfolio-snapshot protection
  • Clear separation between current live state and saved evidence
PULSE COPILOT

Decision support from every page.

Ask what needs attention, what is blocked, why PULSE believes it and what should happen next. Answers are grounded in the current signed-in PULSE state, expose their reasoning and route the SE back to the right workspace. Copilot remains read-only and never bypasses a guarded action.

  • Portfolio-wide priority and blocker questions
  • Evidence, attribution and readiness explanations
  • Contextual next actions from every page
