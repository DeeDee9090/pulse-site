WEEKLY MILESTONES

Prove the work before writing the update.

Weekly begins with an exact reporting period and retrieved evidence. Each substantive item is approved, excluded or routed to Weekly / MSX, Account holding or Connect before PULSE generates milestone commentary.

  • Four-step retrieve, review, generate and update workflow
  • Direct, Partner, Internal and Cross-Territory provenance
  • Safe account holding when genuine pipeline attribution is unavailable
  • Guarded previews for comments and HVA tasks, including duration

FY27 CONNECT

Prepare the conversation—not just the prose.

PULSE discovers the First or Next Connect schedule, builds the correct evidence window and keeps goals, workstreams, contribution type, financial context and MSX attribution visible before generation.

  • Scheduled conversation and pre-read deadline
  • Needs-review, attribution-pending and verified-history queues
  • Five-item pagination with goal, date and workstream filters
  • Private, versioned narratives grounded in verified evidence
