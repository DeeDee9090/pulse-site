★ Weekly Milestones

Retrieve, review, then write the week.

Choose an exact period and PULSE retrieves relevant meetings, email, Teams activity and files in your signed-in Microsoft 365 context. Review what it found, route each item to the right opportunity and milestone, and keep only the evidence that genuinely supports the update. Already-captured evidence is identified so it is not presented as a new candidate or added twice.

 

★ Edit & Push to CRM

Review, edit, push to CRM.

Every card opens into a full editor — refine the weekly progress, set the milestone status, capture next steps and add tasks, and watch the CRM comment build live in the preview. One click writes the forecast comment back to MSX and logs your tasks as activities. The text is a starting point; the final word is always yours — nothing goes to CRM until you click Push, and only Direct customer evidence is eligible to push.

 

★ Portfolio & Opportunity Workspace

Your portfolio, then the complete opportunity.

PULSE maps the signed-in user's aligned portfolio and keeps cross-territory deal-team work distinct. Open an opportunity to see milestones, HVA, evidence-driven HoK recommendations, attach coverage, evidence, ownership and Technical Close readiness together — without losing the account hierarchy or the reason each action is being surfaced.

 

★ SE Execution Tracker

Personal action, team decisions and discovery—kept separate.

Tracker evaluates one signed-in Stage 2–3 population through three deliberately different views. This Week ranks the work the SE can progress now; Opportunity Review isolates recovery, orchestration and escalation decisions for the team; and Opportunity Radar ranks signals that deserve evidence-backed investigation without pretending discovery is already an execution commitment.

★ Market Context Research

Public signals and Microsoft context, clearly separated.

From a selected opportunity, PULSE can run bounded customer research across cited public reporting and private Microsoft whole-account activity available to the signed-in user. It returns fit, why now, evidence used, missing evidence, suggested moves, discovery questions and the next step — and if there is no material signal, it says so instead of inventing a play.

 

★ Guided Technical Close

Turn readiness into a living close plan.

Stage 2 opportunities stay visible as Plan ahead; Stage 3 opportunities without a plan become Start now. The guided workspace preserves readiness, evidence, sections, versions and handover while keeping the signed-off plan separate from the due-dated Technical Close/Win Plan HVA. Existing saved content remains authoritative and is never silently overwritten.

 

★ PULSE Copilot preview

Decision support that knows where you are.

Open Copilot from any PULSE page and it adapts to that workspace, your selected portfolio and the active conversation. Ask about status, risks, evidence gaps or the next best action; when you explicitly ask it to search Microsoft 365, it can retrieve relevant work evidence and distinguish what is already captured from genuinely new candidates. This is an evolving decision-support preview, so verify consequential conclusions before acting.

 

★ CAF Nominations

Never miss a Factory nomination.

PULSE watches the Cloud Accelerate Factory data and surfaces the opportunities that are eligible but not nominated, stalled, or declined. When one qualifies, it assembles the full nomination packet — laid out in the same sections as the Azure Offer Navigator form, with the specialist and co-sell partner auto-resolved — for you to review and submit. PULSE never submits for you.

 

★ FY Connect

Prepare the conversation—not just the form.

PULSE brings the scheduled Connect context, goal targets, performance calculations, manager inputs, workstreams and reviewed evidence into one preparation flow. It drafts reflect-and-plan responses in the user's voice, maps claims to the supporting proof, highlights unresolved inputs and saves private, versioned snapshots so later portfolio changes do not rewrite what was true at the time.

★ Reports & Durable History

Keep the evidence and decision that existed at the time.

Reopen a saved Weekly period or private Connect version with its originating evidence and snapshot context intact. PULSE keeps saved history separate from today's changed portfolio so an earlier decision is not silently regenerated from newer data.

 

★ Built to be trusted

PULSE prepares; you approve.

Nothing touches MSX without your say-so:

✓  Guarded previews show the exact intended MSX operation before confirmation.
✓  Per-user isolation — your reports and evidence are keyed to you alone.
✓  Confirmed writes execute natively as the signed-in user.

🚀 Getting started

Request access, then open the Power App.

There is no agent package, sideloading step or one-time link code:

Access and licensing: PULSE only shows the scope available to the signed-in user. You need an active Microsoft work account, a valid entitlement to run the Production Power App and Dataverse, and an active MSX identity with the permissions needed for the records and actions you use. An MSX seat alone does not grant PULSE access.

1.  If you do not already have access, request PULSE Production Access. The request is approved automatically and adds you to the governed Production user group.
2.  After the assignment has propagated, open the PULSE Production Power App. The app link does not redirect unassigned users to My Access.
3.  Select the automatically discovered Microsoft work account if prompted, accept any one-time connector consent, then start in Weekly, Tracker, Portfolio, Connect, Reports or PULSE Copilot.

See the complete workflow, safety model and Connect guidance in the user guide.
