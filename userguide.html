Your identity matters: reads and guarded MSX writes use your signed-in identity. PULSE does not substitute an owner identity for your own user context.

Access, licensing and lifecycle

  • Initial access: any Microsoft internal member can request the PULSE access package. Eligible requests are approved automatically.
  • What the request grants: Entra adds the user to PULSE Production Users; the group supplies the Power App, Dataverse, flow and agent runtime access.
  • Licensing: users need an active corporate account, a valid entitlement to run the Production Power App and Dataverse, and an active MSX identity with the permissions required for the records and actions they use. An MSX seat alone does not create PULSE or Power Platform access.
  • First launch: accept any one-time delegated connector or WorkIQ consent prompt while signed in with your own Microsoft work account.
  • Renewal: access lasts 365 days. Users can request a self-service extension; otherwise the ELM-managed assignment expires and Entra removes the group access.
  • Current pilot: existing pilot members remain unchanged while the new access package is tested; future joiners should use the access package.

4 · Weekly Milestones

Weekly is a four-step evidence workflow. It does not begin by writing commentary—it begins by proving what happened and deciding where that work belongs.

Step 1 — Select dates and retrieve

  1. Select the fiscal year and a completed reporting week. Use a custom range only for catch-up and keep it within the allowed window.
  2. Select Refresh portfolio and retrieve. PULSE refreshes the signed-in MSX snapshot and runs evidence retrieval for those exact dates.
  3. Use the retrieval status card to see progress, coverage and any explicit diagnostic.

Step 2 — Review and route evidence

For each substantive item, choose the correct destination:

  • Weekly / MSX — qualified customer activity linked to a genuine opportunity and active milestone.
  • Account holding — real customer work where the account is known but legitimate pipeline does not yet exist.
  • Connect — material impact that should contribute to Connect rather than customer pipeline.
  • Exclude — irrelevant, passive, duplicate or incorrectly attributed activity.
No pipeline guessing: never attach evidence to a convenient but unrelated opportunity. Account holding preserves genuine customer work until the account team qualifies the correct opportunity.

Evidence classifications

  • Direct — customer-facing activity with direct customer participation or evidence.
  • Partner — work delivered with or through a partner.
  • Internal — internal work that materially advanced or unblocked the customer outcome.
  • Cross-Territory — contribution to customer work outside your aligned territory.
  • Unclassified — not ready for guarded MSX routing until you choose the correct classification.

Classification explains provenance; it does not automatically veto a legitimate MSX update. Every classified item still goes through explicit preview and confirmation.

Step 3 — Generate the draft

After approving evidence, generate the Weekly draft. PULSE consolidates duplicate signals, keeps supporting context beneath the material contribution, and drafts milestone progress from the verified evidence.

Step 4 — Review milestone updates

  • Filter by territory, customer, health, commitment and evidence source.
  • Review progress, decisions, blockers, next steps and evidence lineage.
  • Create active HVA tasks with an explicit duration (15–120 minutes, default 30).
  • Use the Technical Close prompt when a Stage 2 or Stage 3 opportunity requires a living plan.
  • Select Review milestone updates to preview the exact guarded MSX operations.
Nothing writes silently. PULSE shows the account, opportunity, milestone, comment and task operation before confirmation. Direct creation reads the task back to verify the stored duration.

5 · PULSE Tracker

Tracker is the execution workspace. It evaluates one evidence-led Stage 2–3 population, then presents that signed-in scope through three deliberately different views: personal action, team decision and discovery.

Set the Tracker population before interpreting it

  1. Confirm the selected fiscal year and operating model.
  2. Choose the territory lens that matches the work you are doing.
  3. Refresh Tracker after material MSX changes so the cards use the latest retrieved state.
  4. Read each card’s population and evidence wording. The three tabs use the same relevant opportunity population but answer different questions.
  • My aligned territory — the accounts and opportunities aligned to your saved territory model.
  • Cross-territory deal team — opportunities where you contribute through deal-team membership outside the aligned patch.
Do not combine the two lenses mentally. Aligned territory explains owned portfolio execution; cross-territory deal team explains work where you are contributing outside that patch. PULSE keeps both visible without presenting them as the same accountability.

Confirm the operating profile

PULSE derives the operating model from the signed-in user's current RAIN Qualifier 2. The profile label and source appear above Tracker and in Connect.

  • CAIP — Data, Infrastructure, Apps & AI, Software Development and general Cloud & AI profiles retain the existing discipline rules and validated scorecards.
  • AIBS — Copilot, Apps & Agents, Finance & Supply Chain, Sales & Service, Cloud Endpoint, CCaaS, Business Process, Customer Experience and generic AIBS profiles receive specialisation-specific operating actions.
  • Security — receives Security technical validation, delivery, Technical Close, usage and handoff actions plus validated Connect role guidance.
  • Unknown or unsupported — PULSE fails closed. It does not inherit CAIP rules, targets or scorecards merely because no exact profile was found.

This Week — what must I personally do?

The default tab turns measurable execution gaps into a prioritised personal queue. Focus First ranks the highest-value opportunities with unresolved actions; one opportunity can legitimately appear in several categories because each category represents a different gap.

  • Team coverage — join the relevant opportunity or milestone team when you are actively contributing.
  • Factory action — review Factory-eligible milestones and either act or record the governed exception.
  • POC/Pilot ownership — take ownership of the genuine active technical milestone or create one only when a new phase of delivery has actually started.
  • Technical Close — plan ahead at Stage 2 or start the guided plan at Stage 3.
  • Database and Security attach — assess applicability, follow the surfaced motion or record the explicit governed exception.
  • HVA evidence — add a due-dated High Value Activity to the correct owned active POC/Pilot milestone.
  1. Open the highest-priority segment.
  2. Read the card’s reason, not only its category. Confirm whether PULSE has evidence of a real gap or is explaining a prerequisite.
  3. Open the exact Opportunity Workspace.
  4. Complete the guarded signed-in-user action, or leave the item open if the required customer or MSX evidence does not yet exist.
  5. Refresh Tracker and confirm that the action moved because the authoritative underlying state changed.

Opportunity Review — what needs a team decision?

Use this tab in Scrum, pipeline, account, forecast or manager review. It brings together ownership, milestone-team coverage, POC/Pilot state, HVA evidence, Technical Win coverage, attach posture and orchestration prerequisites, then separates the result by the kind of response required.

  • Talk about together — recovery, ownership, sequencing, escalation or cross-team orchestration needs a shared decision.
  • Do yourself — the signed-in user can complete the next action without consuming team-review time.
  • PULSE cannot tell yet — ownership, milestone, timing or applicability evidence is missing. Treat this as an evidence question, not a confirmed execution failure.

Open the card during review, confirm the accountable person and next decision, then use the Opportunity Workspace for the actual governed change. Tracker does not silently reassign ownership or modify team membership.

Opportunity Radar — what deserves investigation?

Radar helps discover or qualify work that is not yet represented correctly. It applies the selected role focus to the signed-in portfolio, preserves the difference between opportunity and pre-opportunity evidence, and does not force premature pipeline creation.

  • All signals — the complete relevant Radar population.
  • Discipline review — records whose current technical alignment needs confirmation.
  • Qualification — customer need or opportunity representation requires an explicit qualification decision.
  • Prioritised candidates — the strongest current evidence and timing for investigation.
  • Other candidates — potentially relevant work with lower urgency or incomplete evidence.
  1. Select the required role focus and filter.
  2. Read the evidence, represented-pipeline state, timing and reason the candidate surfaced.
  3. If genuine pipeline already exists, link or continue through the real opportunity rather than creating a duplicate.
  4. If a customer need is still unconfirmed, keep it in discovery and use research to prepare the conversation.
  5. After customer confirmation, follow the normal MSX qualification process. Radar itself does not create pipeline.

Research customer and Microsoft context

Use research when a Radar signal is plausible but the “why now”, wider customer context or internal coordination picture is incomplete.

  1. Select Research customer + Microsoft context on the exact Radar candidate.
  2. PULSE runs bounded public research and retrieves the Microsoft whole-account context available to you as the signed-in user.
  3. Review the evidence classification:
    • Publicly reported evidence includes the source, publisher, date and link.
    • Microsoft internal context can include other MSX workstreams, related milestones, named owners and recent Microsoft 365 activity.
  4. Review the resulting motion fit, why-now statement, cited signal, whole-account context, evidence used and evidence still needed.
  5. Use the suggested moves, discovery questions, anchor, next step and talk track to prepare—not to claim the customer has agreed.
  6. Confirm the need with the customer before creating or materially changing pipeline.
Evidence boundaries matter. Microsoft-internal coordination context must not be copied directly into CRM notes or customer communication. If no material cited signal is found, PULSE reports that result and does not invent an engagement action.

Execution measures and scorecard

The numeric scorecard below currently applies only to supported CAIP profiles. AIBS and Security numeric measures are withheld until authoritative targets, populations and calculations are validated.

  • Milestone engagement — relevant milestone team coverage.
  • POC/Pilot ownership — opportunities with an active POC/Pilot you own.
  • High Value Activities — owned active POC/Pilot milestones with HVA evidence.
  • Technical Win coverage — opportunity value supported by a Technical Win plan.
  • Uncommitted to Committed — milestone progression with SE engagement.
  • Database, Security and Factory attach — each uses its stated authoritative denominator and exception rules.

The FY27 scorecard supports current-quarter, completed-quarter, year-to-date and weekly-progress views. Read the denominator on every card; PULSE deliberately avoids mixing different populations into one percentage.

6 · Build a Technical Close Plan DETAILED

Technical Close turns opportunity and milestone evidence into one customer-centred plan per eligible SE-owned POC/Pilot. The guided builder keeps customer outcome, decision criteria, architecture, proof, value, risk and CSU acceptance connected without pretending the plan is complete before the evidence exists.

When the plan appears

  • Plan ahead keeps Stage 2 opportunities visible before the close becomes urgent.
  • Start now identifies Stage 3 opportunities without a saved plan.
  • POC/Pilot ownership and team gaps remain visible as prerequisites; PULSE does not hide the Technical Close need merely because those prerequisites are unresolved.
  • Each eligible SE-owned POC/Pilot has its own independent plan. Do not merge separate technical delivery phases into one generic opportunity plan.

Before starting

  1. Open the exact Opportunity Workspace from Tracker or Portfolio.
  2. Confirm that you are on the relevant opportunity and that the correct active POC/Pilot exists.
  3. Confirm that the POC/Pilot and intended Production milestone have the required ownership and team coverage.
  4. Use Prepare from opportunity evidence where available, then review every prepared value. Prepared content is a starting point, not automatic customer confirmation.

The eight guided stages

  1. Set up — select the SE-owned POC/Pilot and related Production milestone. Review the prepared opportunity brief, industry, customer priorities, Microsoft footprint, competitive environment and ownership coverage.
  2. Customer outcome — write the outcome in the customer’s language. Record the KPI baseline, target, target date, executive sponsor and compelling event. A product deployment is not a customer outcome by itself.
  3. Customer decision — identify the Economic Buyer, Technical Decision Maker and relevant stakeholders. Add the customer’s decision criteria and weights; the completed criteria must total 100%.
  4. Solution & scope — retain the governed architecture link or approved file, then define integrations, security and identity patterns, delivery scope, explicit exclusions and deferred decisions.
  5. Proof & value — link each proof activity to a decision criterion. Record the hypothesis, pass rule, owner, date and evidence. Add the expected workloads, consumption measures, timing and trigger for value realisation.
  6. Risks & actions — record each material technical risk, impact, mitigation or trigger and accountable owner. If no material risk exists, confirm that explicitly rather than leaving the section ambiguous.
  7. CSU handover — name the CSU recipient, agreed scope, acceptance conversation, acceptance state and remaining actions. Every unresolved action needs an owner.
  8. Review & save — inspect the readiness checks, resolve missing gates, confirm the technical disposition and Severity-1 state, then save an explicit version at the required checkpoint.

Drafts, versions and readiness

  • Private working draft — changes autosave to the latest private draft while you work.
  • Explicit saved version — create a version when the exact state is ready for review, sign-off, a major customer checkpoint or handover.
  • Evidence refresh — newly prepared evidence must not silently overwrite existing autosaved or saved content. Review and adopt it deliberately.
  • Readiness checks — customer outcome, decision criteria, architecture, proof, value, risk, disposition and accepted handover remain individually visible.
  • Technical disposition — record the current decision honestly. A plan cannot be treated as technically closed while a Severity-1 risk remains unresolved.

Sign-off, handover and the closing HVA

  1. Review the saved version with the relevant customer and Microsoft stakeholders.
  2. Record the agreed technical disposition and resolve or explicitly retain any blocking risk.
  3. Complete the CSU acceptance evidence, including recipient, scope, date, state and remaining actions.
  4. Create or complete the due-dated Technical Close / Win Plan HVA in MSX through the guarded workflow.
The plan and the HVA are separate lifecycle records. The plan contains the durable technical decision and handover evidence. The HVA records the accountable MSX activity used to drive and close that work.

7 · Portfolio and Opportunity Workspace

  • Browse parent and child account relationships without collapsing legally distinct organisations.
  • Open an opportunity workspace to inspect milestones, team membership, ownership and attached execution evidence.
  • Join eligible opportunity or milestone teams through guarded signed-in-user actions.
  • Review POC/Pilot ownership before taking or creating ownership.
  • Create the correct SE-owned POC/Pilot only when a genuine new phase of technical delivery has started.
  • Use explicit exception reasons and the required FY27 hashtag only when the attach is genuinely not applicable.

Join or leave an opportunity or milestone team

  1. Open Portfolio, select the account, then open the required Opportunity Workspace.
  2. Select Join this deal team, Leave this deal team, Join milestone team or Leave milestone team.
  3. For an opportunity-level action, choose whether to change the opportunity team only, one selected milestone team, or both.
  4. Review the exact opportunity, milestone and scope. Joining can affect Role-Based Incentive attribution, so confirm only when you are actively contributing.
  5. Select Confirm and join or Confirm and leave. MSX evaluates the request using your existing permissions.
Self-only protection: PULSE can add or remove only the signed-in user. It cannot remove the previous owner, change another person’s membership or bypass MSX permissions. Leaving a team removes your selected membership; it does not delete the opportunity, milestone, evidence or another user’s access.

Record a genuine Factory, Database or Security exception

  1. Open the exact attach decision and select Record genuine opt-out only when a positive attach or approved Factory nomination does not exist.
  2. Choose Check PULSE snapshot, Search Microsoft 365 or Enter grounded reason. Fresh search checks Email, Teams and Meetings asynchronously and resumes without duplicating completed streams.
  3. Review every returned source card, including source type, activity date, metadata and deep link. PULSE keeps the evidence summary read-only.
  4. Review the editable detailed reason. Make concise, Improve wording and Strengthen from sources may rewrite the proposal, but AI cannot confirm or write it.
  5. Confirm that the decision is a genuine exception. This enables the exact append-only MSX preview only.
  6. On the separate confirmation screen, inspect the exact comment, any latest teammate warning and the destination before writing as yourself.
Silence is not an exception. A missing workload, absent customer reply or incomplete snapshot is a reason to retrieve or clarify evidence—not a reason to remove ACR from the measure.

8 · Connect — complete workflow DETAILED

Connect is not a one-click prose generator. It is a preparation workflow that combines the scheduled conversation, verified evidence, FY27 goal measures, financial context, workstreams, learning and your agreed forward position.

Role-aware guidance

CAIP continues to use its established FY27 guidance and calculations. Supported AIBS and Security profiles use their own authoritative goal wording and evidence signals. PULSE does not invent numeric targets, and an unsupported profile can continue reviewing evidence without receiving an incorrect goal set or generated narrative.

Conversation and evidence window

  • PULSE discovers the First or Next Connect conversation date and shows the pre-read deadline.
  • First Connect uses the FY start through the conversation date; Next Connect begins after the previous completed conversation.
  • Coverage is assessed only through the last completed Sunday. The open current week and future dates are not shown as missing.
  • Use custom dates only when you deliberately need a different evidence window.

Preparation context

The page keeps personal evidence separate from wider organisational context. Dashboard colours can inform the discussion, but must not be represented as an individual performance score.

  • Review the recommended conversation flow and the FY27 Connect/SMART-goal source guidance.
  • Confirm the required goal details and exactly three business bets.
  • Review the financial and execution summary, customer ACR and HVA/Technical Win records.

Needs your review

Only genuinely new, material activity appears for review. Routine attendance, preparation and repeated programme lineage remain supporting context rather than separate achievements.

  1. Choose the contribution type.
  2. Choose the related FY27 goal or mark it as supporting evidence only.
  3. Choose the Connect workstream.
  4. Verify or exclude the evidence.

The section collapses automatically when there is nothing to action. Detailed evidence lists show five items per page.

MSX attribution pending

These are genuine customer contributions held safely at account level because a valid opportunity or milestone was not available at the time of review.

  1. Select Link MSX opportunity on the exact item.
  2. PULSE carries that evidence record directly into the destination wizard—there is no need to remember its original week.
  3. Choose Weekly / MSX, confirm the real account, then choose the genuine opportunity and active milestone.
  4. If no legitimate opportunity exists, leave it in Account holding. Do not force an attribution.

Verified evidence history

Filter history by from/to date, contribution type, related goal and workstream. Use Clear filters to reset. Five results are shown per page with Previous/Next controls.

Goals, workstreams and learning

  • Open each FY27 goal to see its target, actual, calculation and supporting verified evidence.
  • Select Add manager input to open and focus the exact target field that needs an agreed value.
  • Select Review affected opportunities to open the specific opportunities and reasons behind a scorecard exception.
  • Goal 2 attach evidence must have structured Factory, Database or Security proof or an explicit user confirmation.
  • Consolidated workstreams keep related achievements together and preserve recurring-programme lineage without inflating contribution counts.
  • Role/technical learning is kept separate from mandatory compliance evidence.

Generate and save

  1. Review all evidence, goal measures and required manager inputs.
  2. Select Generate Connect with AI.
  3. Review each answer before changing its depth. The default is Balanced · Recommended.
  4. If one answer needs adjustment, choose Concise, Balanced or Detailed beneath that question and select Regenerate this answer.
  5. PULSE creates a new immutable version containing the regenerated answer while preserving the other three answers and the original evidence snapshot.
  6. Edit the narrative so it reflects your voice and agreed position.
  7. Saved Connects remain private and grouped by period. The latest version is prominent; earlier versions stay available in a compact expandable history.

10 · Feedback

Feedback gives every SE a simple, private way to report a problem, suggest an improvement or request clearer guidance without needing to understand the engineering workflow behind PULSE.

Submit feedback

  1. Open Feedback from the main menu and select Send feedback.
  2. Choose the feedback type, add a short searchable title, and explain what you expected and what happened.
  3. Add any useful detail and up to three privacy-reviewed PNG or JPG screenshots. Do not include customer-sensitive information that is not needed to understand the issue.
  4. Review the automatic page and application context, then submit. PULSE records the request against your signed-in identity.
Production is the real feedback channel. Submit genuine feedback in the Production Power App. UAT records are synthetic validation data and are not the source of truth for real user requests.

Understand the status

My Feedback uses plain-language stages rather than engineering terminology:

  • Received — PULSE has recorded the request.
  • Under review — a reviewer is assessing the evidence and deciding the next action.
  • Action needed — the reviewer needs more information from you. Open the request, read the message and send your response.
  • Accepted — the request has been approved for implementation.
  • Being worked on — engineering work is underway.
  • In validation — the change has reached UAT for controlled validation.
  • Released — the verified change is available in Production.
  • Closed or Already reported — the reviewer explanation remains visible on the request.

Know when something changed

  • A red numbered badge appears on the Feedback menu when one or more of your requests has a new update.
  • Updated cards show New update. Select View update to read it and clear that request’s unread state.
  • The unread state is stored in PULSE, so it remains consistent across browser sessions and devices.
  • Your own submission or response does not create a false notification; meaningful reviewer, engineering, validation and release changes do.

Reviewer controls stay separate

Standard users see only their own requests in My Feedback. Reviewer decisions, secure evidence and internal engineering links are kept in the role-protected Feedback Review workspace, available to authorised reviewers through Health.

11 · PULSE Copilot

PULSE Copilot is an evolving, read-only decision-support preview. The drawer now shows the current page, the current scope and a plain-language explanation of the context it is using before the user asks a question.

Its behaviour is deliberately bounded:

  • Page aware: prompts, placeholder text and governed findings change between Home, Weekly, Tracker, Portfolio, Connect, Reports, Feedback and Health.
  • Profile aware: Tracker and Connect answers use the detected CAIP, AIBS or Security boundary and do not substitute CAIP targets for another role.
  • Scope aware: a named customer or opportunity takes priority; otherwise Copilot uses the user’s selected aligned portfolio and excludes cross-territory accounts unless they are explicitly requested.
  • Conversation aware: short follow-ups continue the active topic, objective and timeframe. Navigating to a new page starts a new logical thread so stale page context is not silently reused.
  • Evidence aware: explicit Microsoft 365 search requests can invoke WorkIQ retrieval. Results distinguish evidence already captured in PULSE from genuinely new candidates, preventing duplicate add recommendations.
  • Relevance bounded: Copilot receives the governed findings relevant to the active page and question rather than mixing unrelated Weekly, Tracker, Connect or Technical Close signals.
  • Read only: Copilot can explain status, evidence gaps, blockers and next actions, but it cannot perform or claim an MSX write.

Example questions:

What needs my attention across PULSE, and why? Which retrieved evidence still needs review or attribution? Where are TCP, HoK, CRM or ownership actions unresolved?

Copilot supports the decision; it does not replace the governed PULSE workflow. Verify consequential conclusions before acting.

12 · Safety, identity and trust

  • Signed-in-user execution: WorkIQ retrieval and MSX writes execute as the user taking the action.
  • Explicit approval: guarded previews show the intended operation before any external write.
  • No pipeline guessing: account holding is preferred to a false opportunity attribution.
  • Evidence lineage: material contributions retain the underlying meeting, email or thread context.
  • Per-user isolation: the app loads the signed-in user’s portfolio, evidence and saved outputs.
  • AI review: generated content remains a draft until the user verifies it.
  • Owner-only adoption: privacy-minimal usage, management-team and operating-profile coverage is restricted to PULSE owners and is not shown to standard users.

FAQ

Do I need the old PULSE agent package or a link code?

No. Those instructions are retired. Use the Production Power App link and your work account.

Why is customer activity in Account holding?

PULSE confirmed genuine work but could not safely link it to legitimate pipeline. Link it later when the correct opportunity exists.

Why can’t I see a milestone?

Check the account and opportunity hierarchy, active status, discipline relevance and your opportunity/milestone deal-team membership.

Will PULSE write to MSX automatically?

No. External actions require a preview and explicit confirmation and execute as the signed-in user.

Why does Connect show evidence but not count it as an attributed customer outcome?

Confirmed evidence can remain MSX-attribution pending until it is linked to a genuine opportunity and milestone.

How will I know whether my feedback has changed?

The Feedback menu shows a red unread count. Open the request and select View update to read the latest message or status and clear the notification.
