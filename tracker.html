THREE WORKSPACES, THREE DIFFERENT DECISIONS

Tracker does not collapse every signal into one queue.

The fiscal year, operating model and territory lens are shared, but each tab answers a different question. Users can switch between their aligned territory and cross-territory deal-team work without mixing the two populations.

1 · This Week — what must I do?

Focus First ranks the highest-value opportunities with unresolved personal actions. Team membership, POC/Pilot ownership, HVA, Factory, Database, Security and Technical Close remain separate action categories so the user sees the exact gap and its reason.

2 · Opportunity Review — what needs a team decision?

Use the cards in Scrum, forecast, pipeline or account review. PULSE separates “talk about together”, “do yourself” and “cannot tell yet”, preserving missing ownership or milestone evidence instead of mislabelling uncertainty as poor execution.

3 · Opportunity Radar — what deserves investigation?

Radar ranks opportunity and pre-opportunity signals by the SE’s role focus, represented pipeline, evidence and timing. Filters separate discipline confirmation, qualification, prioritised candidates and lower-urgency candidates. Discovery remains distinct from execution.

MARKET AND WHOLE-ACCOUNT CONTEXT

Research the signal before turning it into a sales motion.

Research customer + Microsoft context runs two bounded evidence paths together: cited public reporting about the customer and private Microsoft whole-account activity available to the signed-in user.

  • Cited customer signals: title, summary, publisher, publication date, confidence and source link stay visible.
  • Microsoft context: other MSX workstreams, milestones, named owners and recent Microsoft 365 activity expose overlap or duplication.
  • Evidence classification: public and Microsoft-internal context remain visibly distinct; private coordination context is never copied directly to CRM or customer communications.
  • Actionable output: PULSE returns fit, why now, evidence used, missing evidence, moves, discovery questions, an anchor, next step and suggested opener.
  • No evidence, no invented action: if no material cited signal is found, PULSE reports the coverage result and does not generate a play.
THE SAFEGUARDThe result is a research-grounded engagement approach. Customer confirmation is still required before acting.

GUIDED TECHNICAL CLOSE

Build the plan one customer and technical decision at a time.

Stage 2 opportunities remain visible as Plan ahead; Stage 3 opportunities without a plan become Start now. POC/Pilot ownership and team gaps remain visible as prerequisites rather than hiding the opportunity from the Technical Close population.

1 · Set up

Confirm the SE-owned POC/Pilot, related Production milestone, prepared opportunity brief and ownership coverage.

2 · Customer outcome

Review customer position, business outcome, KPI baseline and target, sponsor and compelling event.

3 · Customer decision

Record the Economic Buyer, Technical Decision Maker, supporting stakeholders and customer-weighted decision criteria.

4 · Solution & scope

Retain a governed architecture source and define delivery scope, integrations, identity pattern, boundaries and deferrals.

5 · Proof & value

Link proof activities to decision criteria, define pass rules, owners and dates, then record workloads, consumption measures and triggers.

6 · Risks & actions

Maintain technical risks with impact, trigger or mitigation and accountable owner; explicitly confirm when no material risk exists.

7 · CSU handover

Name the recipient, scope, conversation date, acceptance state, unresolved actions and accountable action owners.

8 · Review & save

Resolve readiness gates, confirm disposition and Severity-1 risk state, then create an immutable saved version.

TCP LIFECYCLE AND CONTROLS

A living draft, explicit checkpoints and a separate closing HVA.

Private autosavePULSE continuously preserves the latest working draft; the user does not need to create noisy manual versions while editing.
Saved versionsCreate a version only when the exact state is ready to review, share, sign off or hand over.
Readiness gatesCustomer outcome, criteria, architecture, proof, value, risk and accepted handover remain independently checkable.
Separate MSX activityThe signed-off plan and the due-dated Technical Close/Win Plan HVA remain deliberately separate records.
Evidence remains reviewable. PULSE can prepare sections from linked reviewed or verified evidence, but it identifies those values as starters. Existing saved or autosaved content remains authoritative and is not overwritten silently.
